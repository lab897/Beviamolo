Beviamolo for Linux

The app for your wine cellar: photograph the label and let the AI recognise the wine, keep every bottle in its place and find the right wine for your dish.

Install with one click

Install Beviamolo

It opens in GNOME Software or KDE Discover. Updates then arrive like for your other apps.

From the terminal

Install

flatpak install --user @URL_SITO@/beviamolo.flatpakref

Open

flatpak run dev.lab897.beviamolo

Update

flatpak update

The app uses the GNOME runtime, which Flatpak downloads from Flathub during the first installation.

Packages are signed and the key is already included in the install file. To check it yourself: beviamolo.gpg

Your account

You need a free Beviamolo account. Searching the wine catalogue is free; label recognition and food pairing use AI credits, and every account gets a free monthly allowance.
